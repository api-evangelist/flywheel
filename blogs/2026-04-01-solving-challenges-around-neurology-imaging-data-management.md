By Pablo Velasco, Senior Scientific Solutions Engineer at Flywheel In neurology, medical imaging data provides invaluable insights into brain structure and function. But neuroimaging researchers face challenges in discovering, aggregating, standardizing and analyzing that data efficiently. Here, we detail some of the top challenges in neurology research regarding imaging data management—and how researchers can solve them to enable new breakthroughs in brain sciences.
